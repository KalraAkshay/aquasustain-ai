What do these inputs mean?

IT capacity: power capacity of servers, storage and networking, excluding cooling and building systems.

Average load: average use of that capacity over the year.

WUE: litres of onsite water consumed per kWh of IT electricity. Lower means less water per unit of computing energy.

Reclaimed share: assumed fraction supplied by treated reused water. Availability needs verification.
